# Shetu demo video script (target: 2:45, hard cap 3:00)

A shot-by-shot script for the submission video. Keep it under three minutes.
Voiceover (VO) lines are written to be read at a calm pace; on-screen action is
in brackets. Times are cumulative targets.

---

## 0:00 - 0:20 · The problem (hook)

**VO:**
"Every year, millions of students try to study abroad. Most start with no
reliable information, so they trust an agency. And too many of those agencies
profit from that trust: they promise guaranteed visas, hide the real costs, and
charge for things that are actually free."

**On screen:**
- Open on the Shetu landing page (`shetu.mol.la`).
- Quick, calm scroll showing the headline: *"Plan your studies abroad with
  honest guidance."*
- Optional: a one-line on-screen caption: *"The students hurt most have the
  least access to honest advice."*

---

## 0:20 - 0:35 · What Shetu is + sign in

**VO:**
"Shetu is an AI advisor built to be the honest, independent voice those students
never had. You just sign in and talk to it."

**On screen:**
- Click **Continue with Google**, complete the sign-in quickly (pre-pick the
  account so it's one click).
- Land on the chat screen with the starter prompts visible.

---

## 0:35 - 1:15 · The killer demo: the scam check (Cost & Scam Guard)

**VO:**
"Here's the moment that matters. A student is told an agency will guarantee their
admission and a full scholarship, for a big upfront fee."

**On screen:**
- Click the starter card **"Is this agency offer legit?"** (or type it).
- Let the response **stream**. As it streams, point out (with a cursor or a
  subtle highlight) the "Checking real costs and red flags" indicator, this is
  the specialist being called.

**VO (over the streaming answer):**
"Watch what it does. It names the red flags, no one can guarantee a visa or
admission; official scholarships are applied for directly and for free. Then it
gives the real, itemised cost, tuition, living costs, insurance, and the visa
proof-of-funds, and every figure is tied to an official source."

**On screen:**
- Scroll gently through the answer so the cost breakdown and the cited sources
  are visible. Pause a beat on a citation.

---

## 1:15 - 1:45 · Honest guidance, not sugar-coating (Readiness / Matcher)

**VO:**
"It isn't just defensive. Ask it to assess your readiness, and it gives an honest
read, realistic options and the gaps you still need to close, not false comfort."

**On screen:**
- New message: *"I have a 3.2 GPA and IELTS 6.0, aiming for a master's in data
  science. How ready am I, and what should I fix?"*
- Let it stream; show the "Assessing your readiness" / "Matching programmes"
  indicators. Scroll to show the prioritised action list at the end.

---

## 1:45 - 2:15 · Vault + document coaching

**VO:**
"Students can keep their documents in a private vault, and have the agent review
them. And they always keep their own originals, never handed to an agency."

**On screen:**
- Go to **Vault**, upload a short sample statement of purpose (`.txt`).
- Back in chat: *"Please review the statement of purpose in my vault."*
- Show the agent reading it and giving structured feedback (show the first few
  bullet points of feedback).

---

## 2:15 - 2:35 · Human mentorship + the architecture point

**VO:**
"When a decision is too big for a chatbot, Shetu arranges a real human mentor.
And under the hood, this is a multi-agent system built with the Strands Agents
SDK: an orchestrator delegating to specialists, where the agents can only state
facts that come from verified, cited sources, so they can't make things up."

**On screen:**
- In chat: *"I'd like to talk to a human mentor about choosing between two
  offers."* Show it confirming and recording the request.
- Cut to the **Mentorship** page showing the request logged.
- Brief cut to the architecture diagram (from the README) for 2-3 seconds.

---

## 2:35 - 2:45 · Close

**VO:**
"Shetu. An honest bridge to studying abroad, so no student has to rely on someone
who profits from keeping them in the dark."

**On screen:**
- Return to the logo / landing page.
- End card: the Shetu logo + `shetu.mol.la` + "Built with the Strands Agents SDK
  on AWS."

---

## Recording tips

- **Pre-seed the demo** so nothing waits: sign in once beforehand so Google is a
  single click; have the sample SOP file ready on the desktop; if possible warm
  up the model once so the first response isn't the slow cold one.
- **Keep responses short on camera.** If an answer is long, scroll rather than
  wait for every token; you can also trim dead air in editing.
- **Show the citations and the "which specialist is working" indicators** at
  least once each, they are the proof points for the honesty and multi-agent
  claims.
- **Record at 1080p**, browser zoom ~110-125% so text is readable on small
  screens.
- Keep it **under 3:00**. If tight, trim the Vault section first (2:15 segment).
